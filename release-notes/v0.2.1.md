First public release.

- Discord status with your character, elite spec, map and total WvW kills
- Hover the image to see your next Realm Avenger tier
- Elite spec icon, play timer, and options to hide each line
- API key stored encrypted for your Windows account
- Updates automatically through Nexus

**Install:** put `GW2WvWPresence.dll` in your `Guild Wars 2\addons` folder, load it in Nexus, and paste a GW2 API key (account + progression) into its options.
